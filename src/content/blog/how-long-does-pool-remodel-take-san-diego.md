A pool remodel does not have one reliable universal duration. A resurfacing-only scope moves differently from a project involving tile, coping, decking, plumbing, equipment, and features. Product availability, access, inspections when applicable, discoveries during preparation, and weather can also affect sequencing.

The most useful way to plan is by phase rather than by an unsupported promise of a fixed number of days.

## Typical project phases

### 1. Scope and selections

The project begins with measurements, condition review, priorities, material selections, and a written scope. Decisions left open at this stage can delay ordering or create changes later. Use the [pool remodel checklist](/blog/pool-remodel-planning-checklist) to gather information before requesting an estimate.

### 2. Scheduling and pre-construction coordination

Materials, crew sequence, access, utility considerations, and permits or inspections when applicable are coordinated before physical work starts. Ask who is responsible for each item and what must be completed before the pool is drained.

### 3. Drain and preparation

The existing condition becomes clearer as finishes are removed or prepared. If concealed damage changes the scope, it should be documented and approved before additional work proceeds.

### 4. Trade work and finishes

Plumbing or feature changes happen in the sequence required by the project, followed by tile, coping, decking, interior finish, or equipment work as applicable. Connected surfaces need coordinated transitions.

### 5. Fill and startup

Finishing the installation is not the same as completing startup. Confirm who manages filling, initial water care, equipment operation, and homeowner instructions. Fresh finishes can have specific startup requirements.

## Questions to ask about the schedule

Ask what the proposed timeline assumes, which selections must be final, how weather or inspections are handled, what could pause work, and how schedule changes are communicated. A range with stated dependencies is more useful than a precise promise without conditions.

## Prepare the rest of your property

Discuss gate and driveway access, pets, landscaping protection, noise expectations, material staging, water discharge, and when the yard or pool area should not be used. If ongoing service needs to pause or restart, coordinate that separately.
